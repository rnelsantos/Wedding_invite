The Wedding Guest Discount & Payment Terms

Alta D’ Tagaytay Hotel offers a special rate for our attendees — here’s how to claim it

  • Guests receive an exclusive 25% discount off the published room rates.
  • To avail of this discount, guests must inform the reservation team that they are booking for our wedding and provide the couple’s surname and the wedding date.
  • To secure the reservation, the hotel requires at least a 50% deposit of the total payable amount.
  • The remaining balance can be paid on the day of check-in.
  • Please note that the discount is not valid during blackout dates, and standard terms and conditions apply.

Good to know

Room Inclusions & Extra Guest Fees

It’s helpful to know exactly what you’re paying for, especially if you’re bringing families or groups

  • Each room accommodation comes with breakfast included for two people.
  • The standard room rate is good for two adults and a maximum of two kids who are 12 years old and below.
  • Guests are provided free use of the swimming pool, and select rooms also include gym access.
  • A maximum of four adults is allowed per room.
  • There is an additional charge of Php 1,500.00 per night for each extra adult.
  • This extra person fee includes breakfast and a floor mattress for the 3rd and 4th person.
  • All rates are inclusive of service charges and applicable taxes.
  • Rates are subject to change without prior notice.
