A few little things to note

Our Little Reminders

Please Arrive on Time

Doors open at 2:30 PM. The ceremony begins promptly at 3:00 PM. We’d love to see all your smiling faces before we walk down the aisle!

Gift Guide

Your presence is truly the greatest gift! If you’d like to give something, we truly prefer a monetary gift — we can’t quite imagine how we’d bring physical gifts all the way from the Philippines back to Ireland! E-wallet transfers are warmly welcomed.

Adults-Only Celebration

We’ve curated an intimate adults-only celebration. We lovingly ask that little ones stay home so everyone can fully relax and enjoy the evening. We appreciate your understanding!

Unplugged Ceremony

We kindly ask that you put away phones and cameras during the ceremony. Be fully present with us — our photographer will capture every beautiful moment so you can simply enjoy the magic.

No Unannounced Plus Ones

Our guest list has been carefully prepared to ensure every guest has a seat. Please do not bring additional guests unless they are explicitly included in your formal invitation. Thank you!

Keep Our Invitation Private

We kindly ask that you keep our wedding invitation details private. Please refrain from making any surprise announcements or publicly sharing our wedding details without our knowledge. Let’s keep it intimate and special.
